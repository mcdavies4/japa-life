Privacy

Last updated 9 October 2026

The short version: no accounts, no emails, no ads tracking you, no cookies. We count how the game is used with a random number, never your name. You can turn that off in the game.

Who we are

Abroad Life (including Japa Life and the japa calculator) is a free browser game made by The 36th Company Ltd in the United Kingdom. Contact: azubuikedavies@gmail.com · @zubidavies on X.

What stays on your device

  • Your game save (your character, money, progress) is stored in your browser’s local storage.
  • Settings like sound, graphics and who you’ve blocked.
  • A random ID (for example k3j9x2p0qa) used for anonymous stats. It isn’t linked to your name.

“Start a new life” in Phone → Settings deletes your save. Clearing your browser data removes all of it.

What we collect, and why

  • Anonymous usage stats: things like “a shift was worked” or “a calculator result was shared”, with your random ID. We use them to see what players enjoy and to show advertisers total numbers. Turn them off in Phone → Settings → Anonymous usage stats.
  • Live play: when you play online, your nickname, look and position are sent to other players on the same street. They aren’t stored.
  • Chat: messages are passed to players nearby and not stored. Links and phone numbers are removed automatically to stop scams.
  • Reports: if someone reports a player, we keep the report and that player’s last few messages for 30 days so we can review it.
  • Cloud saves: your game save is backed up under a random save code (for example K7QM2-XP9FD) so you can continue on another device. There’s no name or email attached.
  • Calculator: your answers stay in your browser. We only count the type of result (for example “broke by month 6”), not your savings or salary.

Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: running the game, keeping it safe and improving it.

Who runs it

The game is hosted on Cloudflare (Pages and Workers), which processes data on our behalf. We don’t sell data or share it with advertisers. Billboard ads in the game are images; they don’t track you.

Being kind, and age

Chat is for ages 13 and over. No insults, sexual messages, begging for money or scams. Tap any player to block or report them. Players reported by several people are removed for an hour.

Your rights

You can ask to see or delete data linked to your save code or random ID. Email azubuikedavies@gmail.com or message @zubidavies on X with your save code. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
