Put sold billboard images here (1024 x 384 px, PNG or JPG).
Then in index.html, find "const ADS={};" and add the slot, e.g.:
const ADS={"ryelane-0b":{brand:"Brand Name",line:"Short tagline",bg:"#0E7A4B",em:"🍛",img:"/ads/brand.png",url:"https://brand.com"}};
Slot ids: <street>-<0 or 1><a or b>. Tap any billboard in the game to see its id.
